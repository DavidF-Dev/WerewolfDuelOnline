Cards and the plan screen: redesign options

Design options for discussion, in the Moonlit theme. All cards share one rounded-card outline; faction is shown by the outline colour, the faction icon (top right) and the art panel's pattern, never by colour alone. Layout follows common card conventions (initiative top-left, name top-centre, faction top-right, ability at the bottom). Art is placeholder glyphs; ability wording and icons are our own.

A · Character cards

Same content, three sizes: large (hand, inspect), medium (reveal/showdown) and small (target list or compact hand). Each card: initiative and name the same size along the top, faction icon top-right, a placeholder art panel, an icon row, and the ability in our own words.

B · The plan screen

Three ways to choose a character and its targets on a phone, each fitting 360 × 740 with no scrolling. The opponent strip, round bar and phase bar are the same in all three.

C · Home, simplified

"Play on this device" and "Rejoin with PIN" are removed from Home: Join game handles PIN rejoin, and local play becomes a choice inside New game.

D · Round 2: ability pills and a fixed art panel

Each ability gets its own pill, with a small gap between pills. The art panel is now a fixed size on every card, so the glyphs line up across a hand; the ability text takes the space below. Three ways to show an attack, since the arrow was easy to miss:

Targets as smaller versions of the character cards, without the ability text (hold to inspect). Two arrangements at real phone width:

F · Round 2: B1 with the new cards

G · Round 3: attack and gain pills

Two kinds of pill, following the physical game's three ideas (attack, gain, spend a marker for an effect) with our own icons. Gain pills are green with the marker gained. Attack pills are red: any marker spent, then ▸, then one faction shape per target, then extra damage after a colon. Effects read left to right in the order they happen.

H · G3 with marker tokens

Every marker sits on a round token, wherever it is drawn: in pills, in card text, in the marker counts and in the game log. Hearts are Health, not markers, so they stay bare.

B1 again, now with the chosen card design: hand along the bottom, targets as small cards with pills, marker tokens in the counts.
